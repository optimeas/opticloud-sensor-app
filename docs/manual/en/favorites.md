# Favorites

The **Favorites** tab opens pages of the optiCLOUD web application right in the app, already signed in: dashboards, devices, and lists. With one tap you get from the phone to the data it sends.

## What the tab shows

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| at the top | The default dashboard. If none is set, the **optiCLOUD Home** page. |
| **In optiCLOUD** | The favorites you created in the optiCLOUD web application, in the same order |
| **On This Phone** | Pages you added in the app |

You change the favorites from optiCLOUD in the web application; the app only shows them. It reads them each time you open the tab and when you pull the list down. Without a network it shows the last state.

Without a sign-in the tab shows only the first entry.

## Adding favorites on the phone

Tap **+** at the top right and choose:

- **Dashboard …**: a dashboard of the project you are signed in to,
- **Device …**: a device of this project, of any type,
- **Device of This Phone**: the device this phone sends to,
- **Dashboard List** or **Device List**: the lists in optiCLOUD.

With **Edit** you change the order and delete favorites. The favorites on the phone belong to the server and the tenant; when you sign in to another tenant, you see its favorites.

Touch and hold a favorite from optiCLOUD to take it over with **Add to This Phone**.

## Default dashboard

Touch and hold a dashboard and choose **Set as Default Dashboard**. It then appears at the top. With **Remove Default Dashboard** on the first entry the app shows the optiCLOUD Home page there again.

The default dashboard can also be set through the shared attribute `dashboard` of the device (see [The device in optiCLOUD](device-in-opticloud.md)). It can then not be changed on the phone.

## Opening pages

A page of optiCLOUD opens in the app, signed in with your account, in the tenant and the project you are signed in to in the app. You do not have to sign in again.

- With **Back** and **Reload** at the bottom you move within the page; **Done** closes it.
- **Open in Safari** opens the current page in Safari. There you sign in yourself.
- The app keeps nothing of the page on the phone. When you close it, the sign-in in it ends.
- A favorite that leads to another website opens in Safari.
- A device of another project or a page of another tenant does not open.
