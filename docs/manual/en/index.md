# optiCLOUD Sensor

optiCLOUD Sensor turns your smartphone into a sensor for optiCLOUD, the IoT platform by optiMEAS. The app measures, for example, motion, air pressure, location, battery level, and sound level, and sends the values as telemetry to a device in your optiCLOUD tenant. There you see them like the data of any other device: in dashboards, alarms, and reports.

This lets you try optiCLOUD without connecting hardware of your own, and use a smartphone as a mobile measuring point – on a drive, during an inspection, or simply on your desk.

## What the app does

- Sign in with your personal optiCLOUD account and pick a tenant and a project.
- Create a device for the app or pick an existing one, and bind it to this phone.
- Send sensor values at an adjustable send rate, also in the background with the screen locked.
- Buffer values without a network and deliver them as soon as the connection is back.
- Accept commands from optiCLOUD and let optiCLOUD manage settings through shared attributes.
- Count its own data volume, separately for the mobile network and Wi-Fi.
- Open dashboards, devices, and your optiCLOUD favorites in Safari with one tap.
- Show a diagnosis log and export it.

This manual describes the app for iOS. The app for Android follows.

The app is open source under the MIT license. Why we do this is explained in [Why open source](open-source.md).

- An optiCLOUD account with access to a tenant and the permission to create or use devices.
- An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
